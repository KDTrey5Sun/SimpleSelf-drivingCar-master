DQN Replay Buffer Research Questions Design
Overall RQ
How does replay buffer size shape sample efficiency and training stability in value-based deep reinforcement learning for a racing task?
 
Experiment 1: Replay Buffer Size (Buffer Size Only)
Experiment 1 RQ
How does replay buffer size alone impact sample efficiency and training stability in DQN?
Experiment 1 Sub-Research Questions
SRQ1.1: How does buffer size affect sample efficiency (convergence speed)?
•	Metrics: first_success_episode, samples_at_first_success, convergence_speed
•	Hypothesis H1.1: Small buffers → faster initial convergence (less data to process); large buffers → slower convergence (more stable but delayed)
SRQ1.2: How does buffer size affect training stability and variance?
•	Metrics: success_rate_variance, avg_reward_variance, avg_loss_variance (across seeds)
•	Hypothesis H1.2: Small buffers → high variance (limited diversity); large buffers → low variance (diverse replay pool)
SRQ1.3: What is the optimal buffer size for balancing efficiency and stability?
•	Metrics: pareto_frontier of (convergence_speed, stability)
•	Hypothesis H1.3: Optimal buffer size exists in the middle range—balancing diversity and computational efficiency
Experiment 1 Design
•	Independent Variable: Replay buffer size (e.g., 10K, 50K, 100K, 500K, 1M)
•	Fixed Variables: Batch size (constant, e.g., 32), all other hyperparameters constant
•	Dependent Variables: Sample efficiency metrics, stability metrics, final performance
 
Experiment 2: Replay Buffer Size × Batch Size (Interaction)
Experiment 2 RQ
How do replay buffer size and batch size interact to shape sample efficiency and training stability in DQN?
Experiment 2 Sub-Research Questions
SRQ2.1: Does batch size moderate the effect of buffer size on sample efficiency?
•	Metrics: first_success_episode, samples_at_first_success (across buffer_size × batch_size combinations)
•	Hypothesis H2.1: Large batch + small buffer → severe overfitting; small batch + large buffer → stable but slow; optimal pairing exists
SRQ2.2: How does the buffer_size:batch_size ratio affect training dynamics?
•	Metrics: avg_loss_variance, training_stability_score, learning_curve_smoothness
•	Hypothesis H2.2: High ratio (large buffer, small batch) → smoother updates; low ratio (small buffer, large batch) → noisy updates
SRQ2.3: What buffer-batch combinations optimize the efficiency-stability trade-off?
•	Metrics: Pareto frontier of (convergence_speed, stability) across all combinations
•	Hypothesis H2.3: Optimal combinations follow a principle (e.g., ratio ≈ 1000:1 or buffer_size ≥ batch_size × N)
Experiment 2 Design
•	Independent Variables: 
o	Replay buffer size (e.g., 10K, 100K, 500K, 1M)
o	Batch size (e.g., 8, 16, 32, 64)
•	Design: Factorial design (all combinations) or subset based on computational budget
•	Fixed Variables: All other hyperparameters constant
•	Dependent Variables: Sample efficiency, stability, final performance
